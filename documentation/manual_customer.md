# Project Manual: Customer

Welcome to the Pench Customer Portal Manual. This guide explains how to manage your subscriptions and orders.

## 1. Login
- Use your registered mobile number and verify via OTP.

## 2. Managing Subscriptions
- View your active subscription plans (e.g., Daily Milk, Alternate Day Water).
- **Pause/Resume**: Pause your delivery if you are away.
- **Modify**: Change the quantity of items for your next delivery.

## 3. Placing One-Time Orders
- Browse the product catalog.
- Add items to your cart.
- Select a delivery date and pay securely.

## 4. Tracking & Notifications
- Receive an SMS/Push notification when the driver starts the trip.
- View real-time tracking of the delivery vehicle on the map.
- Get a confirmation photo once the item is delivered.

## 5. Billing & Payments
- View your ledger and transaction history.
- Download invoices for past orders.
- Pay outstanding balances via integrated payment gateways (Razorpay/Paytm).
