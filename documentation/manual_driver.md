# Project Manual: Driver

Welcome to the Pench Delivery Mobile Manual. This guide explains how to use the app to fulfill your daily deliveries.

## 1. Getting Started
- **Login**: Use your registered phone number. You will receive an OTP via SMS.
- **My Route**: Once logged in, tap "My Route" to see your assigned deliveries for today.

## 2. Starting a Trip
1. Open your assigned route.
2. Tap **"Start Trip"**. This notifies the customers that you are on your way.
3. The map will show the optimized sequence of stops.

## 3. Completing a Delivery
For each stop:
1. Arrive at the customer location.
2. Tap **"Submit Delivery"**.
3. **Proof of Delivery (POD)**: If enabled, you must take a photo of the delivered items at the customer's door.
4. **Bottle Return**: Enter the number of empty bottles you collected from the customer.
5. Tap **"Confirm"**.

## 4. Finishing the Day
- Once all stops are completed, tap **"Complete Trip"**.
- Your performance stats (distance covered, bottles collected) will be updated automatically.

## 5. Support
If you face issues with a location or an order:
- Tap the "Call Support" button in the app.
- Or use the internal chat to message the ERP admin.
