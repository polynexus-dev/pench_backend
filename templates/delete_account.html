Request Account and Data Deletion

At Pench (Pench Logistics & Pench Food), we respect your right to control your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and remove your associated personal information from our system, you can submit a deletion request following the steps outlined below.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account and data deletion using any of the following methods:

Method 1: In-App Deletion Request

  1. Open the Pench App (Customer or Driver Mobile Application).
  2. Go to Account / Profile Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account & Data".
  4. Confirm your request. Your account will be queued for processing immediately.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request (Web Browsers)

If you no longer have the app installed, you can submit an account deletion request via email:

Official Support Channel

Send an email from your registered email address to:

Email: privacy@pench.in or support@pench.in

Subject Line: Account Deletion Request - [Your Mobile Number / Username]

Details to Include: Your Full Name, Registered Mobile Number, and City/Region.

Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

1. Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account Credentials: Username, hashed passwords, session tokens, and security profile.
  • Personal Profile Data: Full Name, Email address, primary phone number, avatar, and personal preferences.
  • Saved Addresses & Geo-Pins: Saved delivery addresses, house numbers, landmarks, and precise GPS coordinates.
  • Active Subscriptions: All recurring delivery schedules, custom frequencies, and vacation pause logs.
  • App Notifications & Device Tokens: Firebase push notification tokens and device IDs.

2. Data Retained for Legal & Financial Compliance

Under applicable Indian statutory, tax (GST), and financial regulations, certain transaction records cannot be immediately erased upon account deletion:

  • Financial Invoices & Payment Records: Tax invoices and billing history are retained for the statutory retention period (typically 6-8 years) as required by accounting and tax laws.
  • Completed Order Audit Logs: Historical order logs (anonymized where applicable) retained for audit and legal compliance.

Retention & Processing Timeline

Upon receiving your account deletion request, your profile will be deactivated immediately, and all eligible personal data will be permanently deleted from our primary servers within 30 days.

Need Help?

If you have any questions regarding your data privacy or the account deletion process, please reach out to our team at privacy@pench.in.
